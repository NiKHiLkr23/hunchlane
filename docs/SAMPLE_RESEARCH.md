## Editorial decisions

- Scope: compare the proposed research-to-build-to-management journey against four alternatives. This is not an exhaustive market study.
- The founder supplied the full product vision. The proposed first segment, differentiation, advantages, risks, pilot design, and success gates are hypotheses or proposals.
- Build, deployment, management, and post-launch learning inside Hunchlane are planned, not demonstrated capabilities.
- No interviews, payments, retention data, or independent market-size evidence was collected. Do not substitute competitor marketing metrics for Hunchlane demand or market size.
- The suggested ten-founder pilot is exploratory; five people per path cannot establish statistical superiority. Its thresholds are selected decision gates, not benchmarks or observed outcomes.
- Static monthly financial assumptions: $49 price; $4 research + $12 building/revisions + $4 hosting + $5 support per customer; $500 fixed costs. Contribution is $24; 21 customers cover entered costs. These amounts are neither provider quotes nor announced prices. Acquisition, founder salary, taxes, refunds, and churn are excluded.
- The public sample and its Markdown export include the same roadmap, strategy, pilot, costs, and coverage limitations. Regular reports retain the Numbers coming-soon UI and existing provider contracts.
