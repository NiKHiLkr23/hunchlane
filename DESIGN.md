# Hunchlane

See what exists. Find your angle.

Hunchlane is a calm research desk for someone with their first business or product idea. Use plain language, visible evidence, and useful next steps. Do not imply certainty from search results or portray financial assumptions as predictions.

Web, Next.js, responsive. Off-white canvas, white paper, deep ink, forest teal accent, warm amber for evidence limitations. System sans body text with Georgia editorial headings. No remote fonts or decorative stock assets. Navigation uses a compact branching-lane SVG mark.

Spacing follows 4px increments. Main reading width 1120px; body line length about 65 characters. Inputs and buttons at least 44px high. Focus rings visible. Durations in milliseconds: feedback 140ms, disclosure 180ms. CSS transforms/opacity only; reduced motion removes travel and loops.

Reports separate cited observations, hypotheses, and user-entered assumptions. Sources stay near claims and are clickable. Sample reports always state they are illustrative. Never display fabricated live progress. Public shares contain immutable report snapshots only. Empty libraries show a clear first action. Errors preserve drafts and offer recovery.
