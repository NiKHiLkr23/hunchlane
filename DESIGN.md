# Hunchlane — Teal and Depth

See what exists. Find your angle.

Hunchlane helps beginners explore software, physical products, and services. The design gives an unpolished idea a clear place to start, keeps evidence within reach, and makes uncertainty understandable. Research is context, not a prediction of success.

## Identity and phase adoption

The selected direction uses the user's ChronoTask screenshots for composition and soft depth, with deep teal replacing blue. White surfaces, a pale neutral canvas, rounded panels and soft shadows frame a centered headline and compact idea field. The CoreShift recording informs staged entrances and layered movement. Retain the Hunchlane name, branching-lane mark, and locally hosted Manrope with its SIL Open Font License. The landing now uses realistic thin paper, woven book cloth, glass and metal, with teal details and restrained apricot accents. Natural upper-left lighting and small contact shadows keep the objects tangible; avoid inflated edges and clay materials. Report text and citations remain HTML.

The user approved the complete phase 1 landing and authorized phase 2 login/signup on `codex/teal-motion-landing`. The landing includes the centered hero, responsive header, three original dimensional illustrations, a layered scheduling sample, three benefits, and a closing explanation of uncertainty. `src/app/hero.css` scopes the hero; `src/app/landing-details.css` scopes the lower sections; `src/app/studio.css` retains the shared landing header/form foundation. Earlier designs remain on their branches, including `codex/paper-teal-landing` at `34e8384`. Phase 2 adds a shared authentication composition scoped by `src/app/auth.css`. After approving login/signup, the user authorized phase 3, the signed-in home. `/home` adopts the same system through `src/app/home/home.css`; new-idea, report, and library redesigns remain later phases. Present desktop/mobile home states for feedback before phase 4.

The signed-in home presents one next action before recent work. Its priority is active research, a valid device-local draft, then the latest report. If none exists, invite a first idea; a failed run remains reachable with “View progress.” Show actual saved stage text without simulated percentages, and link to the progress screen for current updates. Recent rows show category, UTC calendar date, saved status, and a refinement label when a parent exists. Report and progress links use the existing owner-checked routes.

A white next-action panel and a smaller sage curiosity panel sit side by side on desktop and stack on mobile. No entrance animation delays content; controls retain 140ms feedback and become static under reduced motion.

- Manrope variable type, locally loaded with `next/font/local`. Hero display 38–64px, weight 600, line height 1.08, tracking -0.04em, centered. The headline reads “Give your idea / a clearer next step.” with a teal final phrase and no underline. It wraps naturally on narrow screens. Supporting copy is quieter and narrower, about 38 characters wide, so the headline carries the hero.
- 4px spacing increments. The landing column is 1040px wide and centered, so wide screens keep a large equal margin on the left and the right. The header uses the same canvas as the page, with no white bar, and shares that column with the hero, partner row, sample, benefits, closing, and footer. The hero is the centered rounded card: an 18px white form at a maximum 540px, and perimeter cards that break the card edge on wide desktop. The card does not use a negative side margin. At 900px, remove the miniature cards and clip the hero; on mobile retain a small idea-note illustration and disable decorative scroll travel. The primary action remains in the first viewport. Directly under the hero, a hairline “Neathacks partners” row links to the public neatHack partners: neatlogs, Entire, cfo.ai, Crustdata, AWS, LiteLLM, and Orchestrator. The row drifts until hover, focus, or reduced motion, which shows each name once. These are event partners, not customers and not evidence the product works.

- Feedback 140ms. Entrances take 480ms with 90ms stagger, opacity and small transforms. The hero mark, headline, and supporting line enter over 480ms; the composer does not move or wait. Isolated client components bound hero travel to 42px and showcase illustration travel to 22px, updating only while visible. The wide alternatives sheet enters first, then the stacked hypothesis and experiment, followed by the benefit sequence and headings. ## Behavior and evidence

The landing form uses the existing idea composer and draft format. Valid submission saves locally and opens `/new`; it never starts research. Examples set the matching software/product/service category. Errors preserve input and focus the field. The mobile navigation is a native disclosure with keyboard activation, Escape close/focus restoration, and closing links.

The scheduling preview derives from the deterministic sample report and is explicitly labeled “Illustrative sample,” with a visible explanation that it is not live research. Product links open the official websites; descriptions and hypotheses do not establish current details or retrieved-page provenance. The sample shows alternatives, a labeled hypothesis, an unanswered question, and a manual experiment. Report-page redesign remains phase 5. Reports separate cited observations, hypotheses, and user-entered assumptions. Citations remain near claims; a receipt is not proof of every interpretation. Public shares expose immutable report snapshots only. Never imply novelty, market demand, financial predictions, or sponsor success without evidence.

Later phases preserve context through authentication and require explicit research submission after review. Libraries distinguish device-local drafts from saved reports. Empty states provide a useful next action; failures preserve work and explain recovery. Paid calls retain reservations and operation records; uncertain charges require reconciliation.

## Phase 4 — A conversation around the idea

The user selected a minimal conversational intake inspired by Codex, replacing the earlier form wizard. `/new` begins with one idea composer, then a thread with user messages aligned right and Hunchlane replies aligned left. Suggested starting points are buttons; a textarea accepts personal answers and uncertainty. A small category choice appears once before intake. Numbers remain optional; existing financial assumptions are retained. The review presents only the user's idea and accepted context, with an explicit Start research action. AI suggestions are unverified and excluded unless selected. Review can be reached with the context already available.

Manrope, white space, deep teal, sage user bubbles, 20px composer corners and 140ms control feedback extend the approved system. No entrance delays or simulated thinking timeline are added. Normal scrolling remains; reduced motion removes spinner rotation and control motion, including on preference changes. Actual research progress polls saved stages every three seconds and displays only updates observed in this visit, without invented source counts or completion percentages.

A versioned v2 device draft stores the brief, current step, accepted conversation, input buffer and uncertain operation identity. Legacy v1 briefs remain readable. Local storage failures remain visible and prevent provider submission. New idea retains an earlier draft until a valid opening is explicitly saved. Reload and login never submit intake or research; both require a deliberate action. Unknown provider outcomes offer readback instead of resending.

The user's request for live AI intake adds a separately prepared server endpoint and zero-funded intake reservation/journal recipe. It remains disabled pending operator budget approval and sponsor pricing/cap confirmation. The isolated `/dev/idea-preview` and `/dev/research-preview` galleries use prominently labeled deterministic fixtures, never account impersonation or paid calls, and return 404 in production. The UI is reviewable while live integration awaits activation; no live intake or trace success is claimed.

Preview conversations use an isolated local-storage namespace through the same draft helpers, so fixture replies never overwrite a real draft or appear in the live intake.
